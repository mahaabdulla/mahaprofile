# Fonts and graphics

Inter, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, and JetBrains Mono are self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The original license texts are distributed in `public/fonts/`.

- Inter: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/inter; the WOFF2 binary comes from https://github.com/rsms/inter/tree/master/docs/font-files
- IBM Plex Sans Arabic: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ibmplexsansarabic
- JetBrains Mono: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/jetbrainsmono

The system Georgia font is referenced as a platform fallback and is not redistributed.

Lucide icons are provided by `lucide-react` under the ISC license distributed with the package.

Project visual compositions, diagrams, and orbital geometry were written for this site in HTML/CSS/SVG. They are illustrative concepts, not screenshots of actual applications. No external stock imagery or reference-site assets are included.
